Who We Are?

Who We Are?

The Rotaract Club of Colombo is the first and oldest Rotaract Club in Sri Lanka, and the third in South Asia. Established on 9th September 1969, we are a registered non-profit social service organisation under Rotary International and fall under the guardianship of the Rotary Club of Colombo (founded in 1929). The Club aims to develop effective leaders and promote responsible citizenship to maintain international understanding and peace. We continue our proud legacy as a group of young professionals committed to personal, professional and community development. Meetings are usually held once a month on Tuesdays.

Our Avenues of Service

Community Service

Community service is a cornerstone of the Rotaract Club of Colombo, reflecting the club’s commitment to addressing the needs of society and fostering sustainable development. As part of Rotary International’s global network, the club carries out various service initiatives aimed at improving the lives of underprivileged communities in Sri Lanka.

Read More

The Rotaract Club of Colombo undertakes a wide range of community service projects, which typically focus on:

  • Education and Youth Empowerment: Organizing book donation drives, conducting free tuition classes, and career guidance workshops for students in underserved areas.
  • Health and Well-being: Hosting health camps, blood donation drives, and mental health awareness programs.
  • Environmental Sustainability: Engaging in tree planting campaigns, beach cleanups, and promoting eco-friendly practices.
  • Disaster Relief and Rehabilitation: Providing aid and support to communities affected by natural disasters, including food, clothing, and medical assistance.
  • Poverty Alleviation: Distributing essential items and facilitating livelihood programs for families in need.

Each project is executed with careful planning, often involving partnerships with local organizations, government bodies, and community leaders. These initiatives not only bring tangible benefits to the community but also instill a spirit of volunteerism, leadership, and civic responsibility among Rotaractors.

By actively engaging in community service, the Rotaract Club of Colombo continues to play a vital role in uplifting society and promoting peace and goodwill, in line with Rotary’s motto: Service Above Self

Club Service

Club Service is a vital avenue within the Rotaract Club of Colombo, focused on strengthening the internal structure and fellowship of the club. Its primary objective is to ensure that members are engaged, motivated, and provided with opportunities for personal and professional growth within a vibrant and collaborative environment.

Club Service activities are designed to build strong interpersonal bonds among members and create a sense of unity and belonging. These initiatives include:

  • Fellowship Events: Organizing social gatherings, cultural nights, movie evenings, and themed parties to encourage camaraderie and team spirit.
  • Member Engagement: Conducting orientation sessions for new members, team-building activities, and regular general meetings to keep members informed and involved.
  • ·  Recognition and Appreciation: Celebrating member achievements through awards, appreciation nights, and shout-outs to recognize contributions and milestones.
  • ·  Club Branding and Identity: Promoting the club’s culture, values, and image through effective internal communication, club merchandise, and social media engagement.

International Service

International Service and International Understanding are key avenues of focus for the Rotaract Club of Colombo, reflecting the club’s commitment to fostering global unity, cultural exchange, and cross-border collaboration. These initiatives aim to build bridges between communities across the world while promoting peace, tolerance, and mutual respect—core values of the Rotaract and Rotary movement.

Read More

Under this avenue, the club undertakes projects and activities such as:

  • Twin Club Partnerships: Collaborating with Rotaract clubs in other countries to execute joint projects, share best practices, and build lasting friendships.
  • Cultural Exchange Programs: Organizing virtual and in-person exchange events to celebrate diverse traditions, languages, and customs.
  • International Celebrations: Hosting themed events around global observances such as International Peace Day, World Rotaract Week, and United Nations Day.
  • Global Projects: Participating in or leading international initiatives that address global issues like climate change, public health, and education, often in partnership with global organizations.
  • Letter Exchange Programs: Facilitating one-on-one cultural exchange between members of different countries to promote grassroots-level understanding.

Through these efforts, the Rotaract Club of Colombo plays an active role in fostering global citizenship, enhancing diplomatic values, and creating opportunities for young leaders to gain international exposure and empathy.

This avenue not only broadens members’ horizons but also strengthens Rotary’s vision of “Creating Hope in the World” by encouraging youth to act as ambassadors of peace and understanding.

Professional Development

Professional Development is a core avenue of service in the Rotaract Club of Colombo, aimed at equipping members with the knowledge, skills, and experiences needed to excel in their careers and personal growth journeys. Recognizing the importance of nurturing future leaders, the club focuses on creating meaningful learning opportunities that go beyond academic education.

Read More

Key initiatives under this avenue include:

  • Workshops and Seminars: Organizing sessions on topics such as leadership, public speaking, financial literacy, time management, entrepreneurship, and digital skills.
  • Career Guidance and Mentorship: Providing access to industry professionals and mentors who offer career advice, resume reviews, and interview preparation support.
  • Networking Opportunities: Hosting panel discussions, speaker series, and mixers where members can connect with professionals, alumni, and Rotarians from diverse fields.
  • Company Visits and Industry Exposure: Arranging field visits to companies and organizations to give members firsthand insights into different work environments and industries.
  • Skills Development Programs: Facilitating training in areas such as project management, negotiation, design thinking, and soft skills to prepare members for the modern workplace.
  • Internship and Job Placement Support: Assisting members in securing internships and job opportunities through partnerships with corporates and alumni networks.

Through these initiatives, the Rotaract Club of Colombo empowers its members to become confident, capable, and socially responsible professionals. The focus is not only on individual growth but also on building a network of young leaders who are prepared to make meaningful contributions to society.

Professional Development underlines Rotaract’s belief in “Learning through Service”, helping members grow both as individuals and as impactful community leaders.

Our Signature Projects

Charity Bus

This Community Service project is a race to complete eight to ten projects in two days out of Colombo, where members spend time with vulnerable communities, make donations, paint buildings, plant trees, and much more!

Escape to Paradise

Rotaractors from across the globe are invited to take part in a tour of hotspots across Sri Lanka to foster cultural exchange and friendship across borders, along with a service element.

Christmas with a Difference

This seasonal staple is a visit to a Children’s or Elders’ home to spend this special day with them and spread the Christmas cheer with songs, food and festivities.
