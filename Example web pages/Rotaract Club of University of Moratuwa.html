Community Service

The future lies in the hands of the people of the community at large. We strive to build communities from the bottom up so that as much of us will have the means to truly persevere together to make our tomorrows that much brighter.

Professional Development

The more you do for others, the more you discover yourself. Rotaract Mora is as much about the enhancement of the professionalism and confidence of the budding youth as it is about serving the world.

Club Service


Rotaract Mora would cease to be if not for its driving life force - the dedication, unity, and kinship of its members. We bring together young individuals from all corners of the country to form one big tight-knit family in which all can rely on one another.

International Service

Be it the West, East, North, or South of the globe, we are all one and the same and merely separated by Earth’s topography. We at Rotaract Mora are advocates of the fact that global service and understanding are key in unifying the entirety of mankind.

Environmental Service

Our relationship with Mother Nature should always be a two-way street; yet we take so much and give so little back. Rotaract Mora is on a mission to change the mindsets of people and thus help allow nature and all its creations to truly thrive in all their glory.

About Rotaract Mora

Rotaract Mora is about serving our kind and the living as a whole, exploring new territory, creating opportunities, and building future leaders. Nurtured by the dynamics of one of the leading technical universities in Sri Lanka, we strive to provide sustainable and novel solutions to societal and environmental issues and share what knowledge we have on the ways of the world with the common masses, in hopes of changing attitudes and directions of lives. We are much more than the typical volunteer organization; our mission is to not only serve others but also to develop ourselves into better human beings through the process.
